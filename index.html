How to print it

  • Print at 100 percent, or actual size. Never "fit to page." Fit-to-page shrinks the sheet and pulls every panel boundary off its crease.
  • Single sided, landscape, US Letter.
  • Any black and white printer is fine. There are no photographs and no gray screens, so a low toner cartridge still reads.

How to fold it

  1. Fold the sheet in half the long way, like a hot dog. Unfold.
  2. Fold it in half the short way, like a hamburger. Fold in half the short way again. Unfold everything flat.
  3. You now have eight rectangles. Cut the one heavy line, which runs along the middle crease across the two center columns only.
  4. Fold hot dog again, then push the two ends toward each other so the cut opens into a cross. Wrap it around into a booklet with the cover on the outside.

Where a printed line and a crease disagree, follow the crease.

Notes

  • The four keys the guide covers sell as one cheap pack online. Search "bait station keys."
  • Know the law where you are before you open or remove anything.
  • Gloves on. Double bag any bait you take out.
